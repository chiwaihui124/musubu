# Musubu — public explainer, plan

Decided 2026-08-20. Planning only; nothing built except one prototype.
Companion to `Musubu Project/README.md` and `Musubu Project/CLAIMS_REGISTER.md`.

---

## What this is, and what it is not

**Not** a platform, a product tour, or a pitch deck. An **explainer of a concept**
— a seed of an idea someone can explore, get curious about, and want to pursue.

Wai's framing: *"a work in progress concept idea that I wanted to share, hoping
there's enough there to spark someone's interest and curiosity."*

The innovation being proposed: borrowing a mechanic that already works elsewhere
(conditional pledges) and pointing it at intentional living, coordination, and
national-scale renewal — combining known good ideas into something new.

## Audience — deliberately broad

Not one persona. Any of these landing would be a win:

- a town, seeing what it could be
- a government official believing it is possible, even in part
- a startup in Fukuoka seeing potential in it
- an ordinary Japanese citizen thinking it is a good idea

Success is momentum and interest, not conversion.

## Language

**Build in English first**, then translate wholesale to Japanese when it is ready
to share — Wai does not read Japanese well enough to develop in it. But the
audience above is largely Japanese, so Japanese is *required before launch*, not
optional. Structure for translation from the start, as Pickleball in Asia does.
A `translator-ja` agent already exists.

## Shape — roughly seven screens

1. **Open on the reframe, not the problem.** Japan already knows its towns are
   emptying; leading with that wastes the opening. Lead with the line:
   *"The choice was never Tokyo versus the countryside. It was Tokyo versus a
   question mark."*
2. **The stuck part.** Not only that moving first is risky — that a town nobody
   can evaluate is not an option, it is a guess. Illegibility, not standoff.
   (See `01_Foundation/musubu_transition_thesis.md`.)
3. **The mechanism.** Conditional commitment, made interactive. **Drafted:**
   `Portal/musubu-mechanism-draft.html`. Facts resolve from blur to sharp as
   commitments accumulate, so "coordination adds specification" is something the
   reader watches happen.
4. **Who would actually do this — the life hinges.** Not yet drafted. The
   strongest unused material in the corpus: people relocate because their life is
   already in motion, so *"you are not draining a pool; you are standing at a
   river."* Also the manufactured-freshman-year argument — nobody is already
   inside. (See `musubu_transition_thesis.md`, `musubu_life_stage_doors.md`.)
5. **What it looks like if it works.** Explicitly a scenario. Wai's own words:
   clusters of intentional communities built through coordination, systems
   thinking, calculated risk, and support to transition to a new life.
6. **What I don't know yet.** The open questions, stated plainly. This is the
   section that turns a pitch into a seed — it makes the reader a potential
   participant. *"If you have input, please share. If you want to use the idea,
   please let me join."*
7. **Get in touch.** Connect, explore it, grow it.

## The claims rule

Because the framing is concept-and-possibility rather than proposal, the
`CLAIMS_REGISTER.md` job simplifies to one rule:

> **Anything Tier 3 or 4 is phrased as *if*, never as *is*.** Scenario language,
> never forecast or prediction. No claim is made as fact.

Small illustrative claims throughout are fine. The prototype already follows
this — "Example Town", labelled illustrative, qualitative values only.

## Build risk

Twelve existing prototypes in `Portal/` will pull toward shipping a product
tour. They are **illustrations inside the argument**, not destinations.

## Resolved 2026-09-17

- **Front door is the scene, not the argument.** `Portal/index.html` opens on the share-house screen (49 of 56) and offers three depths: this concept page at five minutes, the coordination-layer page at seven, the atlas for everything. The concept page keeps its own opening. This follows Wai's own framing on 2026-08-30 and 2026-09-16, both of which began with the portal screen years in.
- **Shape is a stack, not a single artifact.** A deck compresses too far and one town undersells; a stack a stranger enters at any depth does neither.
