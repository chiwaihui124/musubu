# Musubu Project Implementation Roadmap
## From Concept to First Community Launch

---

## Overview

This roadmap takes the Musubu Project from initial concept through first successful community launch in approximately 18-24 months. It's designed to be iterative, learning from each phase to refine the next.

**Guiding Principles:**
- Start with belief before logistics
- Prove demand before building infrastructure
- Community formation before migration
- Test assumptions at each stage
- Multiple small bets rather than one big bet

---

## Phase 1: Validation & Community Building

**Goal:** Validate demand exists and identify early believers

### Week 1-2: Survey Creation & Beta Testing
**Activities:**
- Build national survey (online form)
- Beta test with 10-20 people
- Refine questions based on feedback
- Set up basic website/landing page
- Create social media presence

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Finalized survey ready to launch
- [ ] Simple website with project vision
- [ ] Social media accounts (Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, LINE)
- [ ] Email collection system

**Budget:** ~¥100,000 ($700) - Domain, hosting, survey tools

- Share survey with personal networks

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] First 100 surveys completed

## Phase 2: Town Partnership & Design
### Months 4-6: "Select Locations & Design Communities"

**Goal:** Secure 2-3 pilot town partnerships and design detailed community models

### Month 4: Town Research & Outreach
**Activities:**
- Research potential partner towns (under 10,000 population)
- Criteria: declining population, government openness, existing infrastructure
- Outreach to municipal governments
- Visit 10-15 candidate towns
- Assess infrastructure, school capacity, housing stock

**Target Towns:**
- Town A: Tech Training Village (needs good internet, some existing tech company)
- Town B: Family Growth Town (aging population needing eldercare workers)
- Town C: Creative Hub or Heritage Town (existing craft tradition or natural beauty)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] List of 15 candidate towns researched
- [ ] Initial conversations with 10 municipalities
- [ ] Site visits to top 5 candidates
- [ ] Assessment reports for each

**Budget:** ¥800,000 ($5,500) - Travel, accommodation, materials

---

### Month 5: Partnership Negotiation
**Activities:**
- Negotiate with top 3 town governments
- Secure commitments: land access, zoning support, subsidies
- Identify local partners (employers, schools, community groups)
- Design specific infrastructure plans for each town
- Create detailed budgets

**Key Partnerships Per Town:**

**Tech Training Village:**
- 3-5 remote-first companies (hiring commitments)
- Bootcamp provider or create curriculum
- Co-working space operator
- Government training subsidies

**Family Growth Town:**
- 2-3 eldercare facilities (job commitments)
- Government eldercare training programs
- Childcare provider or school partnership
- Healthcare facility

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] 2-3 towns formally partnered
- [ ] MOU or agreements signed
- [ ] Company hiring commitments (where applicable)
- [ ] Subsidy programs identified and applied for
- [ ] Infrastructure costs estimated

**Budget:** ¥1,000,000 ($7,000) - Legal, consulting, travel

---

### Month 6: Detailed Community Design

**Deliverables:**

- [ ] Visual materials and marketing content
- [ ] Community governance framework
- [ ] Financial model and budget
- [ ] Timeline for launch

## Phase 3: Platform Development & Pre-Launch
### Months 7-9: "Build the Coordination Infrastructure"

**Goal:** Create the technology platform and prepare for commitment phase

### Month 7-8: Platform Development
**Activities:**
- Build or customize commitment platform
- Features needed:
  - Town browsing and exploration
  - User profiles and preferences
  - Conditional commitment system
  - Real-time threshold tracking
  - Community forums
  - Document management
  - Payment/escrow integration
  - Trial visit booking system

**Build vs. Buy Decision:**
- Option A: Custom build (¥5-8M, more control)
- Option B: Customize existing platform like Kickstarter/Eventbrite + forum
- Recommendation: Start with Option B for speed

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Platform built and tested
- [ ] User experience tested with beta users
- [ ] Payment/escrow system operational
- [ ] Mobile responsive
- [ ] Security and privacy compliant

**Budget:** ¥3,000,000-5,000,000 ($21,000-35,000) - Development

## Phase 4: Commitment Phase
### Months 10-12: "Reach Threshold for First Town"

**Goal:** Get first town to 75% commitment threshold (38 of 50 families)

### Month 10: Launch Campaign
**Activities:**
- Big announcement: "Musubu Project Now Open"
- Media blitz (PR, social, paid ads)
- Host launch events in major cities
- Email campaign to survey respondents
- Personal outreach to highly interested individuals
- Daily updates showing commitment progress

**Messaging:**
"For years you've wanted this. Now it's real. Join 50 families building [Town Type] together. Commitment is conditional - you only move if we reach 50 families. See who's already committed. Be part of making this happen."

**Targets:**
- Week 1: 10-15 commitments
- Week 2: 20-25 commitments
- Week 3: 30-35 commitments  
- Week 4: 40+ commitments

**Budget:** ¥2,000,000 ($14,000) - Advertising, events, PR

---

### Month 11: Community Building & Trial Visits
**Activities:**
- Weekly online meetups for committed families
- Begin scheduling trial visits
- "Live Like Local" 2-week programs begin
- Form working groups (childcare, education, community culture)
- Build relationships before move
- Address concerns and questions

**Trial Visit Program:**
- Subsidized 2-week stays in pilot towns
- Meet other committed families
- Experience daily life
- Tour schools, facilities, nature
- Meet potential employers/partners
- Option to back out after if not right fit

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Weekly community calls happening
- [ ] First 10 trial visits completed
- [ ] Working groups formed and active
- [ ] Community culture developing
- [ ] Retention: 90%+ still committed after trials

**Budget:** ¥1,500,000 ($10,500) - Trial visit subsidies, events

---

### Month 12: Threshold Achievement & Planning
**Activities:**
- Celebrate threshold being met!
- Commitments now binding (though still some flexibility)
- Detailed move planning begins
- Housing assignments
- Job/training enrollment
- School registration
- Moving logistics coordination
- Build anticipation and excitement

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] First town reaches 50 families committed
- [ ] All commitments converted from conditional to binding
- [ ] Move date set (typically 6 months out)
- [ ] Individual family plans created
- [ ] Infrastructure construction/renovation begins

**Budget:** ¥500,000 ($3,500) - Celebration event, planning

## Risk Mitigation Timeline

### Month 3: First Go/No-Go Decision
- If <500 survey responses → Pause and refine messaging
- If survey shows no clear demand → Pivot approach
- **Decision:** Continue if validated demand exists

### Month 6: Second Go/No-Go Decision  
- If no towns willing to partner → Find new candidates or adjust model
- If infrastructure costs too high → Scale down or find more funding
- **Decision:** Continue if 2+ towns secured

### Month 12: Third Go/No-Go Decision
- If threshold not reached → Extend timeline or refund commitments
- If trial visits show major issues → Address before proceeding
- **Decision:** Launch only if 50+ families truly committed

### Continuous Risk Monitoring
- Weekly metrics tracking
- Monthly stakeholder check-ins
- Quarterly strategic reviews
- Always have pivot options ready
