# Musubu: Company & Organization Relocation Survey
## "Japan 2040: Is Your Organization Ready to Help Build the Next Community?"

---

## Overview

A companion survey branch targeting companies, organizations, and business owners — probing interest in rural relocation, satellite operations, or talent pipeline participation in Musubu pilot towns.

This solves a core coordination problem: **families won't commit without knowing jobs exist, and companies won't commit without knowing talent is coming.** The survey captures both sides of that equation simultaneously, making the coordination signal visible to everyone.

---

## Three Deployment Options

### Option A: Branch Within the Family Survey
After Q5 (work situation), anyone identifying as a business owner, executive, or HR professional gets routed into a 5–6 question company branch before rejoining the main flow. Captures intent from people who are simultaneously potential movers *and* potential employers.

### Option B: Standalone Companion Survey
A separate "Japan 2040: Business Edition" distributed through:
- Business associations and chambers of commerce
- HR and talent acquisition networks
- Industry groups (tech, eldercare, manufacturing, creative)
- Government economic development channels

### Option C: B2B Proactive Outreach Tool
Musubu sends this to companies in relevant industries as a low-friction first contact — "We're mapping where companies are open to going. 10 minutes?" No formal pitch required. Starts the conversation before a sales relationship exists.

## What This Data Unlocks

### For Towns
A pipeline not just of incoming families but of incoming employers. Changes the municipal conversation from "people are moving here" to "jobs and people are arriving together."

### For Families
Visible proof during the coordination phase that employers have already signaled interest. Dissolves the "but will there be work?" barrier before it forms.

### For Companies
A low-risk way to signal interest and get matched with a pre-committed talent pool — before any formal commitment is required.

### For Government
Aggregate data on which industries are open to rural relocation, at what scale, and under what conditions. Direct input for subsidy program design.

### For Investors
Proof that the demand signal exists on both sides of the marketplace simultaneously.

## Open Questions for Development

1. **Should this be a branch or a standalone?** The branch option captures the overlap (people who are both potential movers and business decision-makers) but the standalone reaches pure B2B contacts who would never take the family survey.

2. **What's the right tone?** The family survey is emotional and validating. The company survey may need to be more direct and ROI-focused — or is there a version that speaks to founders/owners as *people* who also want a different life?

3. **Should companies be able to see family-side data?** Showing a company "3,200 remote workers in your industry have expressed interest in Kyushu" during or after the survey could be a powerful conversion moment.

4. **Anonymity vs. pipeline building:** Companies may be more willing to respond honestly if anonymous, but Musubu needs contact info to build the employer side of the marketplace. How do we balance this?

5. **Timing relative to family survey:** Does the company survey launch simultaneously, or after the family survey has generated enough data to show companies a talent pool already forming?

## Relationship to Platform

The company survey feeds directly into the **Company Portal** on the Musubu platform:
- Expressed interest becomes a platform profile
- Job signals become visible to committed families during coordination phase
- Company × town matching begins during survey phase, not after
- The survey is effectively the company's first step into the coordination process
