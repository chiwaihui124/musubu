# Musubu Platform Roadmap
## Phase 1 (MVP) → Phase 2 → Phase 3+

*High-level phasing of platform features, integrations, and research priorities. Updated April 2026.*

---

## Strategic Framing

Musubu ships as a **coordination platform that works immediately**, then becomes more intelligent over time.

- **Phase 1 (MVP):** Families and towns can commit to each other. The platform is a high-visibility funnel that makes demand legible and removes friction from the commitment process.
- **Phase 2 (Months 12–18):** Platform becomes predictive. Behavioral data improves town-family matching. Intelligence system mature enough to influence government and company strategy.
- **Phase 3+ (Year 2+):** Platform becomes a national coordination infrastructure. Multi-sector optimization (government subsidies, company hiring, town infrastructure planning) driven by Musubu's data.

## Phase 1: MVP Launch (Months 0–12)

### Core Platform Features

**Town Onboarding & Portal**
- [ ] Direction-setting onboarding (town chooses archetype + 8–12 vision questions)
- [ ] Town portal builder (drag-and-drop, template-based, no code required)
- [ ] Portal components: gallery, FAQ, social calendar, basic community info
- [ ] Town coordinator dashboard (commitment tracking, family inquiry management)

**Family Onboarding & Survey**
- [ ] National survey ("Japan 2040") — 33 questions across 5 engagement stages
- [ ] Survey completion → account creation → platform access
- [ ] Family profile creation (life stage, preferences, move readiness, constraints)
- [ ] Town discovery interface (browse by archetype, region, vibe)

**Matching & Commitment Flow**
- [ ] Town favoriting system
- [ ] Inquiry workflow (family → town, town response)
- [ ] Conditional commitment flow (milestone-based commitment with escrow protection)
- [ ] Multi-stage commitment tracking (browse → express interest → pre-commit → deposit → move-in)

**Transparency & Trust**
- [ ] Proof-of-life feed (town-generated moments: events, completions, community signals)
- [ ] Basic composition display (declared direction + "X families have expressed interest")
- [ ] Family testimonials (post-move, 3/6/12 month)
- [ ] Town verification (government partnership confirmation, coordinator credentials)

**Operations Infrastructure**
- [ ] Town coordinator training program (video + live sessions)
- [ ] Family support (onboarding, pre-move checklist, settlement support)
- [ ] In-platform messaging (family ↔ town)
- [ ] Basic analytics for Musubu operations (traffic, survey response rate, conversion funnel)

### Town Archetypes Supported (Launch)

- Family Growth Town
- Youth Energy Hub (remote work-friendly)
- Creative Community
- Eldercare & Wellness Town (if Pilot 1 is Eldercare; otherwise Phase 2)
- Simple Life / Slow Living Town
- Startup Village (if partner company exists)

### Surveys & Research

- [ ] Japan 2040 national survey launch (target: 10,000+ responses in first 3 months)
- [ ] Company outreach survey (concurrent, 500+ company responses)
- [ ] Pilot town baseline research (family + town coordinator interviews, economic baseline)

### Government Alignment

- [ ] Budget mapping complete and presented to MEXT, MHLW
- [ ] 3–5 government partnerships formalized (regional revitalization, eldercare, declining birthrate)
- [ ] Pilot town funding secured (50–70% government coverage)

### Data & Intelligence (MVP Layer)

**Town Dashboard (Limited)**
- [ ] Demand Overview tab (national survey aggregates by town type)
- [ ] Who's Looking tab (life stage breakdown with move-readiness %s)
- [ ] Priorities & Dealbreakers tab (top factors influencing decisions nationally)
- [ ] Direction-setting guidance (infrastructure gaps for chosen archetype)

**Platform Behavior Tracking (Foundation)**
- [ ] Event logging: survey responses, portal visits, favoriting, inquiries, commitments
- [ ] Funnel analytics: how many move from survey → favorite → inquiry → commit
- [ ] Town performance metrics: response time, commitment velocity, satisfaction proxy
- [ ] User analytics: demographics, geography, behavior patterns

**Post-Move Data (First Cohorts)**
- [ ] 3-month check-in survey (settlement, surprise factors, satisfaction)
- [ ] Relationship to proof-of-life feed (what moment types drove commitment)
- [ ] Optional family testimonials (video + text, with incentive)

## Phase 2: Intelligence & Expansion (Months 12–24)

### Platform Intelligence System

**Composition Display (Enhanced)**
- [ ] Live composition charts: life stage breakdown, skills/professions, nationality mix, commitment velocity
- [ ] Why people are coming: word cloud / category breakdown (remote freedom, family space, pace, cost, belonging, etc.)
- [ ] Activity participation rates ("78% attended community event this week")
- [ ] Proof-of-life feed maturity (daily moments that make town feel real)

**Behavioral Data Layer**
- [ ] Revealed preference analysis (where families spend time, which towns get return visits)
- [ ] Tipping point intelligence (what converted interested → committed for successful moves)
- [ ] Segment-level conversion optimization (which tactics work for families with young kids vs. remote workers vs. empty nesters)
- [ ] Town-town comparison (benchmarking for similar archetypes)

**Quarterly Pipeline View**
- [ ] Predictive signals: "20% of committed families will move next quarter"
- [ ] Settled resident retention: "X families have been settled 6+ months, here's satisfaction data"
- [ ] Segment velocity: "Remote workers moving faster than declared; families slower"
- [ ] Town momentum tracking: which towns are accelerating vs. plateauing

**Fit Score Methodology** (Phase 2, not MVP)
- [ ] Define alignment scoring: declared direction vs. revealed interest
- [ ] Calculate per-town + per-family
- [ ] Use for improved matching algorithm
- [ ] Surface divergences as town guidance ("You said families, but you're attracting creatives—want to lean in?")

### Platform Features

**Advanced Matching**
- [ ] Fit score-informed recommendations (families see towns most likely to match)
- [ ] Persona-based discovery ("towns like what you're looking for")
- [ ] Segment-specific comparison (show families similar life situations what they can expect)

**Company Integration**
- [ ] Company portal (view demand signals by town cluster)
- [ ] Talent pipeline signaling (companies pre-commit to hiring in specific towns)
- [ ] Co-op integration (companies sponsor local business formation)

**Caretaker Matching (Eldercare-focused)**
- [ ] Caretaker survey (origin, care specialty, family situation, language, career goals)
- [ ] Caretaker ↔ town matching layer
- [ ] Caretaker onboarding (language support, cultural orientation)

**Adult Children Survey** (Phase 2, not MVP)
- [ ] Separate survey branch targeting adult children making elderly relocation decisions
- [ ] Decision-making flow (elderly preferences + family constraints + town fit)
- [ ] Family communication tools (template emails to parents, group planning tools)

### Ecosystem Expansion

**Expanded Town Archetype Support**
- [ ] Agricultural Innovation Town (co-ops + farming education)
- [ ] Heritage Preserve Town (cultural preservation + multigenerational craft)
- [ ] Healthcare & Wellness Hub (beyond eldercare: preventive health, fitness culture)

**New Integrations**
- [ ] Real estate partnerships (housing inventory integration)
- [ ] Co-operative federation alignment (formal partnership + co-op deployment)
- [ ] Childcare provider network (kindergarten + care infrastructure visibility)
- [ ] Remote work infrastructure vendors (broadband verification, co-working space booking)

### Research & Learning

**Post-Move Longitudinal Study**
- [ ] 12-month data collection from Pilot 1 families + towns
- [ ] Satisfaction drivers analysis
- [ ] What surprised people (positive + negative)
- [ ] Community formation metrics (friendship networks, participation rates)
- [ ] Economic outcomes (vs. pre-move baselines)

**Caretaker Integration Research** (if Eldercare archetype launched)
- [ ] Retention rate vs. national baseline (target: 70%+ at 2 years)
- [ ] Cultural integration effectiveness (food businesses, events, language support impact)
- [ ] Remittance + financial services usage

**Government Value Analysis**
- [ ] ROI to government: subsidy spent vs. economic impact
- [ ] Demographic impact (birth rate effects, population stabilization)
- [ ] Healthcare cost reduction (if applicable)

## Phase 3: National Coordination Infrastructure (Year 2+)

### Platform Evolution

**Clustering & Discovery**
- [ ] Natural clusters visible (5+ towns with similar profiles can be packaged together)
- [ ] Cluster-based discovery ("find towns like what you want")
- [ ] Shared infrastructure playbooks (towns in same cluster share co-op templates, broadband strategies, etc.)
- [ ] Company cluster outreach (B2B: "we have 5 towns all attracting tech talent, hire across them")

**Predictive Infrastructure**
- [ ] Government subsidy timing optimization ("front-load resources here, based on predicted settlement momentum")
- [ ] Company hiring planning ("talent will be available in Q3 from these towns, plan recruitment")
- [ ] Town preparation (housing readiness, school enrollment, clinic staffing)

**Data Moat**
- [ ] Platform becomes harder to replicate: 5+ years of behavioral data, post-move outcomes, proven triggers
- [ ] Becomes default infrastructure for any government rural revitalization program

### Scale & Expansion

**National Coverage**
- [ ] 100+ towns on platform (across all archetypes)
- [ ] 20,000+ families moved through Musubu system
- [ ] 50,000+ survey respondents providing continuous data

**International Expansion** (Exploratory)
- [ ] Southeast Asia (caretaker origin countries): Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia
- [ ] East Asia (similar demographics): South Korea, Taiwan
- [ ] European models (case study + adaptation)

### Public Sector Integration

**Government as User**
- [ ] Regional revitalization ministries use Musubu data to target subsidies
- [ ] Healthcare ministry uses caretaker data for workforce planning
- [ ] Education ministry uses school readiness data from family profiles
- [ ] Immigration office coordinates foreign worker integration via Musubu data

**Regulatory Evolution**
- [ ] Potential pilot-to-policy pathway: successful models become government programs
- [ ] Tax incentives for companies that hire from Musubu towns
- [ ] Caretaker integration becomes part of national foreign worker strategy

## Open Questions by Phase

### Phase 1 (Solve Before Launch)

1. **Survey deployment:** Which channels reach target demographics? Social media budget vs. partnership with media outlets vs. government announcement?
2. **Platform accessibility:** How do we ensure elderly families and non-tech-savvy town coordinators can use this?
3. **Town commitment:** How many towns signed up before launch? What's the risk if fewer than 10 towns are ready at day 1?
4. **Escrow mechanics:** How does deposit protection work legally? Which financial institution partner?

### Phase 2 (Solve in Months 6–12)

1. **Fit score methodology:** Self-assessed by towns, calculated by Musubu from survey data, or both?
2. **Post-move data collection:** Opt-in survey, passive behavior tracking, or structured check-ins?
3. **Caretaker recruitment:** Does Musubu actively recruit, coordinate with existing agencies, or both?
4. **Adult children decision-making:** How does platform mediate when elderly person and adult children disagree?
5. **Composition visibility threshold:** How many data points before live charts are shown (15? 20? 30)?

### Phase 3 (Strategic, Not Critical Path)

1. **International expansion:** Which country first? What's the regulatory pathway?
2. **Government policy integration:** At what scale does government adopt Musubu data as official planning tool?
3. **Company scale:** Do tech companies establish offices in towns, or do they hire remotely?

## Success Metrics by Phase

### Phase 1 Success
- 10,000+ survey respondents
- 5–10 towns live on platform
- 100+ families move through commitment flow
- 50+ families settle in Pilot 1 town
- Pilot 1 town government confidence + media coverage

### Phase 2 Success
- 50,000+ survey respondents (cumulative)
- 30–50 towns active on platform
- 500+ families settled across all pilots
- Fit score improves match quality (measurable satisfaction increase)
- Caretaker retention rate proves model (if Eldercare pilot active)
- 3+ government partnerships formalized

### Phase 3 Success
- 100+ towns, 20,000+ families settled
- Platform data is cited in government planning documents
- 50%+ retention rate at 3+ years post-move
- Natural clustering enables company hiring coordination
- Model replicable in 2+ international markets
