Musubu 結 An open concept · 2026

A share house in a town of four thousand. Forty-nine of fifty-six rooms already taken.

This is a concept for how people could move to Japan's emptying towns together instead of alone. It was worked out in detail by one person over about eighteen months, and it is online so that anyone can read it, take it, or build it.

Nothing here exists yet. Every screen on this site is illustrative.

What is true

Exists
  • A thesis about why people do and do not relocate
  • A specified mechanism, every part labelled by how much is known about it
  • A candidate first town, studied from public sources
  • Economics, housing and co-op models with their assumptions marked
  • A register that tiers every load-bearing number
  • Fifteen prototype screens
Does not exist
  • A single survey respondent
  • A town that has agreed to anything
  • A company, a team, or funding
  • A verified renovation labour cost
  • A legal opinion on holding deposits
  • A day-one version of any screen

Take it

The person who made this would rather it existed than owned it. Everything on this site and in the folder behind it is released for anyone to use, adapt, or build on, with attribution.
