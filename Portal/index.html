日本語で読む

これは、日本の空いていく町へ、一人ではなく一緒に移り住むための構想です。一人の人間が1年半ほどかけて細かく考えたもので、誰でも読んで、持っていって、作れるように公開しています。

5分 構想の全部を、1ページで 本当の問題は何か、仕組みはどう動くか、何が邪魔をしているか、何がわかったか。考えた本人が一人称で書いています。 7分 5年後のMusubuを、画面として 上のシェアハウス、その周りにある町の計器盤、そして人を止めているものを約束できる選択肢に変える、その下の循環。 20分 構想の全体を、八つの部品で 問題、誰が行くのか、仕組みと段階、ポータル、調査、根拠、お金、そしてわかっていないこと。それぞれ、いちばん良いところから始めて、深く読む先を示します。 じっくり すべての文書（英語） 56の文書と68の未解決の問いを1ページに。作業中の資料なので英語のままですが、各文書に日本語の一行説明がついています。

Start here, in English

This is a concept for how people could move to Japan's emptying towns together instead of alone. It was worked out in detail by one person over about eighteen months, and it is online so that anyone can read it, take it, or build it.

Five minutes The whole concept on one page What the problem actually is, how the mechanism works, what is in the way, and what was learned. Written in the first person by the person who made it. Seven minutes Musubu, five years in, drawn as a screen The share house above, the town's instrument panel around it, and the loop underneath that turns what stops people into options they can commit to. Twenty minutes The whole idea, in eight pieces The problem, who would go, the mechanism and its phases, the portal, the poll, the evidence, the money, and what is not known. Each piece leads with its best thing and points deeper. As long as you like Every document Fifty-six documents and sixty-eight open questions on one page, filterable by branch and by how settled each one is. Every number tiered by its source.
Work in progress. This is a living folder, not a finished thing. Documents change, numbers get corrected, and the open questions are open.

Nothing has been proposed to the city and no figure on this site has been agreed with anyone. Everything here describing what could happen is a scenario, not a forecast.
