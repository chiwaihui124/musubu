Musubu town page · Ōita Prefecture

Bungotakada

豊後高田市 · on the Suo-nada sea · 2 hours from Fukuoka, 45 minutes from Ōita City

A river town where the paddy meets the sea, with a Shōwa-era shopping street that never got rebuilt and is now the reason people visit. We have been losing about 1.5% of our population every year since 2005. In 2027 we started doing something about it differently — not advertising for individuals, but coordinating arrivals so that people land together.

What we are asking for

We would rather say this plainly than write a brochure.

Decided by the town assembly in April and reviewed every six months. If what you do is on this list, we will help with housing and the paperwork, and you will not be the only one arriving.

This is what the town is short of. It is not a list of who is welcome. Anyone may move to Bungotakada, and anyone may join the programme — nothing here is a condition of entry. These are the gaps we can actively help you fill, because we hold housing and posts against them. If what you do is not on this list, you are no less welcome; you will just be arranging more of it yourself.
