The mechanisms, drawn.

Seven things this project argues that are genuinely hard to carry in a sentence. Each figure shows a mechanism rather than naming one — where commitments flow, what changes between two options, and which arrow disappears when a piece is removed.

Speculative project material. Figures 1–7 depict mechanisms proposed by the Musubu project; the numbers used as examples are illustrative and unverified. Nothing here has been implemented or agreed with any municipality.
