# The Musubu Project
## Coordinated Migration to Revitalize Rural Japan

---

## Executive Summary

The Musubu Project solves Japan's rural depopulation crisis by addressing it as a **coordination problem** rather than an individual migration problem. By synchronizing family migrations and guaranteeing community formation before anyone moves, we remove the primary barrier preventing rural relocation: fear of isolation.

**Core Insight:** People want rural living but fear isolation. Towns need residents but cannot guarantee community will form. We coordinate commitments to ensure both happen simultaneously.

> ⚠️ **Superseded, August 2026.** Two corrections to this framing live in `musubu_transition_thesis.md`. First, wanting rural life is a **state, not a trait** — people move when their life is already in motion, not because of a stable preference. Second, and correcting the "coordination failure / nobody moves alone" language used throughout this document: **it is not a standoff, it is illegibility.** Nobody is waiting to see whether others will go. Tokyo is simply the only option with a known shape, and a town you have never lived in cannot be assessed from outside. What coordination adds is *specification*, not persuasion. The mechanism below is unchanged; the diagnosis is not.

## The Solution: Coordinated Migration

### The Kickstarter Model for Communities

Just as Kickstarter coordinates buyers to ensure products get made, Musubu coordinates migrants to ensure communities form.

**How It Works:**

1. **Town Selection & Design**
   - Partner with rural towns (population <10,000)
   - Define economic model and infrastructure
   - Set population threshold (typically 50 families)

2. **Conditional Commitments**
   - Families commit conditionally via platform
   - Commitments held in escrow
   - Only activate when threshold is met

3. **Community Formation Before Migration**
   - Forum access for committed families
   - Virtual meetups and planning sessions
   - Trial visits ("Live Like Local")
   - Build relationships before moving

4. **Synchronized Launch**
   - All families move within coordinated timeframe
   - Welcome infrastructure ready
   - Community guaranteed from day one

5. **Long-term Support**
   - Job placement/training as specified
   - Childcare and education infrastructure
   - Community management support

## Risks & Mitigation

### Risk: Threshold Not Met
**Mitigation:** 
- Multiple town types spread risk
- Lower initial thresholds (40-50 families)
- Extensive pre-launch community building
- Media strategy to drive awareness

### Risk: Economic Model Fails
**Mitigation:**
- Diversified town types test different models
- Conservative job projections
- Company partnerships provide stability
- Government subsidy integration

### Risk: Community Conflict
**Mitigation:**
- Clear values/vision alignment upfront
- Conflict resolution training
- Community managers
- Progressive commitment allows opt-out

### Risk: Government/Regulatory Barriers
**Mitigation:**
- Early government engagement
- Leverage existing subsidy programs
- Pilot in friendly municipalities
- Demonstrate economic benefits
