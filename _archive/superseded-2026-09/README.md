# Superseded documents, archived September 2026

Moved here on 2026-09-17 during the consolidation pass that prepared the
corpus for a public reader. **Nothing is deleted.** Every file here was marked
`superseded` in the August 2026 atlas and each has a newer document that carries
its current content. They were archived because a stranger opening
`01_Foundation/` or `02_Platform/` met the retired framing before the current
one, which is the single most likely way for the corpus to contradict itself
in front of a reader.

| Archived file | Superseded by | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| `musubu_project_overview.md` | `01_Foundation/musubu_transition_thesis.md` | The original spine. Its "people want rural living but fear isolation" and "coordination failure / no one wants to move alone" diagnoses were both corrected in August 2026. Its six fixed town types were replaced by four axes in `04_Pilot_Towns/musubu_archetype_axes.md`. Still the best single record of the project's first year of thinking. |
| `musubu_platform_vision.md` | `musubu_platform_vision_v2.md`, then the two below | First platform document. Kept for lineage. |
| `musubu_platform_vision_v2.md` | `02_Platform/musubu_coordination_architecture.md` and `02_Platform/musubu_portal_taxonomy.md` | Largely absorbed. Its "Japan Is Breaking" opening carries Tier 3 and 4 figures stated as fact; see `CLAIMS_REGISTER.md`. |
| `musubu_national_survey.md` | `06_Surveys_and_Outreach/musubu_intake_instrument.md` | The 33-question fixed form. Its welcome text spoke only to families, which the transition thesis identifies as excluding every other life stage from paragraph two. |
| `musubu_survey_strategy.md` | `06_Surveys_and_Outreach/musubu_living_profile.md` | The survey-as-event model, 6,773 words. Replaced by the standing-register model. Its "making invisible desire visible" framing survives inside the living profile. |
