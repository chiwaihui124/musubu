## Survey Introduction

**Welcome**

Thank you for taking the time to share your story with us. This survey is part of the Musubu Project - an initiative exploring how coordinated migration could help Japanese families build the lives they want while revitalizing rural communities.

**Your Voice Matters**

There are thousands of people across Japan who feel stuck - wanting to raise families, build meaningful careers, and live in strong communities, but feeling like these goals are impossible in our current system. This survey is about understanding what you really want, what's stopping you, and what would make change possible.

**How We'll Use This**

Your responses will help us:
- Understand the real challenges families face
- Design communities that meet actual needs
- Remove barriers to making positive change
- Connect people who share similar visions
- Develop concrete solutions, not abstract plans

**Privacy**

Your responses are confidential. We'll publish aggregate results to show patterns and prove demand, but individual responses remain private unless you explicitly choose to share your story publicly.

**Time Required:** 15-20 minutes

Let's begin.

## Part 4: The Possibility

*This section explores a specific solution concept.*

### SCENARIO: Coordinated Migration Project

Imagine this:

**A coordinated migration project where 50 families move together to revitalize a rural Japanese town.** 

Key features:
- You wouldn't move alone - you'd move with a community of people who share your values and life stage
- Jobs, training, childcare, and schools would be established BEFORE anyone moves
- Everyone commits conditionally - you only move if enough people commit, guaranteeing community from day one
- You'd meet other families before moving through forums and trial visits
- Different towns have different focuses (technology training, family support, creative work, eldercare, agriculture, etc.)
- Housing would be affordable (roughly 30-50% of Tokyo prices)
- Government subsidies and support programs integrated
