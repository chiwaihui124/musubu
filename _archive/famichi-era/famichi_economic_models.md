# Famichi Economic Models by Town Type
## Financial Viability Analysis

---

## Overview

Each town type has a different economic foundation. This document analyzes the financial viability, income sources, and sustainability of each model.

**Key Principles:**
- Jobs must exist or be created before families move
- Income must support family life (¥250K-400K/month household minimum)
- Mix of "brought" jobs (remote) and "created" jobs (local)
- Government subsidies reduce risk during transition
- Multiple income streams create resilience

---

## Model 1: Tech Training Village 💻

### Economic Foundation: Remote Work + Training Pipeline

**Target Population:** 60 families (120 adults)

### Revenue/Income Sources

#### Individual/Household Income
**Year 1 (Training Phase):**
- Training stipend: ¥150,000/month (government subsidized)
- Household: ¥300,000/month for dual-adult households
- Part-time remote work allowed: +¥50-100K/month

**Year 2+ (Employment Phase):**
- Junior developer: ¥300-400K/month
- Mid-level developer: ¥500-600K/month
- Senior developer: ¥700-900K/month
- Average household: ¥600-800K/month

#### Community Income Streams
- Training fees from companies (¥1-2M per cohort)
- Co-working space fees: ¥20-30K/person/month
- Local tech services to Tokyo companies
- Some graduates start companies, employ others

### Expenditure/Costs

#### Individual/Household Costs
- Housing: ¥50-80K/month (vs ¥200-300K Tokyo)
- Living expenses: ¥100-150K/month
- Childcare: ¥30-50K/month
- **Total: ¥180-280K/month**

#### Community Infrastructure Costs
**Initial Setup (One-time):**
- Co-working space fit-out: ¥10M
- Training equipment/software: ¥5M
- Childcare facility setup: ¥15M
- Housing renovation (10 units): ¥30M
- **Total: ¥60M**

**Ongoing Annual:**
- Co-working space operations: ¥6M
- Childcare staff (3 people): ¥9M
- Training instructors (2 people): ¥12M
- Community manager: ¥5M
- **Total: ¥32M/year**

### Financial Viability Analysis

**Break-Even:**
- Co-working fees: 60 people × ¥25K × 12 months = ¥18M
- Training fees: 2 cohorts × ¥1.5M = ¥3M
- Government subsidy needed: ¥11M/year ongoing
- **Achievable with standard rural revitalization subsidies**

**Household Economics:**
- Income: ¥600K/month average (year 2+)
- Expenses: ¥230K/month
- Savings potential: ¥370K/month (¥4.4M/year)
- **Highly viable for families**

**Risk Level:** Medium
- Depends on company partnerships for employment
- Requires maintaining tech skill relevance
- Competition from other remote work hubs

**Mitigation:**
- Pre-secure 5+ company hiring commitments
- Continuous curriculum updates
- Build reputation as quality talent source
- Geographic arbitrage remains attractive even if wages compress

## Model 2: Family Growth Town (Eldercare Focus) 👶

### Economic Foundation: Eldercare Services + Government Subsidies

**Target Population:** 50 families (100 adults)

### Revenue/Income Sources

#### Individual/Household Income
**Eldercare Workers:**
- Entry (certified): ¥220-250K/month
- Experienced (3+ years): ¥280-320K/month
- Specialist/supervisor: ¥350-400K/month
- Average household: ¥500-600K/month (dual income)

**Support Services:**
- Teachers: ¥280-350K/month
- Childcare workers: ¥200-250K/month
- Healthcare: ¥300-500K/month
- Local businesses: Variable

#### Community Income Streams
- Eldercare facilities (government contracts)
- Childcare services for community
- Educational services
- Some remote work

### Expenditure/Costs

#### Individual/Household Costs
- Housing: ¥40-60K/month
- Living expenses: ¥100-130K/month
- Childcare: Subsidized/free (community provides)
- **Total: ¥140-190K/month**

#### Community Infrastructure Costs
**Initial Setup:**
- Eldercare facility partnership: ¥0 (existing facilities)
- Training center: ¥8M
- Childcare facility: ¥20M
- Community center: ¥15M
- Housing renovation: ¥25M
- **Total: ¥68M**

**Ongoing Annual:**
- Training program: ¥8M (partially subsidized)
- Childcare operations: ¥12M
- Community services: ¥6M
- Administration: ¥4M
- **Total: ¥30M/year**

### Financial Viability Analysis

**Break-Even:**
- Eldercare facility fees to town: ¥5M
- Childcare fees from workers: ¥6M
- Government subsidies: ¥12M (training, rural support)
- Property tax increase: ¥2M
- Local business tax: ¥5M
- **Total: ¥30M** ✓

**Household Economics:**
- Income: ¥550K/month average
- Expenses: ¥165K/month
- Savings potential: ¥385K/month (¥4.6M/year)
- **Very viable for families**

**Risk Level:** Low
- Guaranteed demand (aging crisis)
- Government committed to supporting eldercare
- Cannot be outsourced
- Multiple facility partnerships spread risk

**Mitigation:**
- Partner with 3+ facilities (not dependent on one)
- Government subsidies very likely (national priority)
- Diversify beyond just eldercare

## Model 3: Manufacturing Town 🔧

#### Individual/Household Income
**Manufacturing Workers:**
- Entry technician: ¥280-320K/month
- Skilled operator: ¥350-400K/month
- Engineer/supervisor: ¥450-550K/month
- Average household: ¥700-800K/month

**Support Services:**
- Teachers, childcare, services: ¥200-350K/month

#### Company Contributions
- Company invests in facility: ¥100-200M
- Pays property taxes to town
- Training subsidies
- Possible housing subsidies for workers

### Expenditure/Costs

#### Individual/Household Costs
- Housing: ¥50-70K/month (company subsidized)
- Living expenses: ¥120-150K/month
- Childcare: ¥30-40K/month
- **Total: ¥200-260K/month**

#### Community Infrastructure Costs
**Initial Setup:**
- Manufacturing facility: ¥0 (company builds)
- Training center: ¥10M (company + government)
- Childcare facility: ¥15M
- Housing: ¥30M (partial company subsidy)
- **Total: ¥55M community investment**

**Ongoing Annual:**
- Childcare operations: ¥10M
- Community services: ¥8M
- Training updates: ¥5M
- Administration: ¥4M
- **Total: ¥27M/year**

### Financial Viability Analysis

**Break-Even:**
- Company facility taxes: ¥8M
- Worker income taxes to town: ¥6M
- Government subsidies: ¥8M
- Service business taxes: ¥5M
- **Total: ¥27M** ✓

**Household Economics:**
- Income: ¥750K/month average
- Expenses: ¥230K/month
- Savings potential: ¥520K/month (¥6.2M/year)
- **Highly viable**

**Risk Level:** Medium-High
- Dependent on company commitment
- Manufacturing sector volatility
- Company could relocate

**Mitigation:**
- Long-term contract with company (10+ years)
- Government guarantees or penalties for early exit
- Diversify skills so workers aren't single-company dependent
- Build multiple company partnerships

- Emerging creatives: ¥150-300K/month
- Craftspeople: ¥200-400K/month
- Teachers/instructors: ¥250-350K/month
- Average household: ¥400-600K/month

**Income Mix:**
- 40% remote creative work (design, writing)
- 30% local craft sales
- 20% tourism/workshops
- 10% teaching

### Expenditure/Costs

#### Individual/Household Costs
- Housing: ¥40-60K/month
- Studio space: ¥20-40K/month (subsidized)
- Living expenses: ¥100-130K/month
- Materials: ¥30-50K/month
- **Total: ¥190-280K/month**

#### Community Infrastructure Costs
**Initial Setup:**
- Studio complex: ¥25M
- Gallery/exhibition space: ¥15M
- Workshop facilities: ¥10M
- Housing renovation: ¥20M
- **Total: ¥70M**

**Ongoing Annual:**
- Studio subsidies: ¥10M
- Gallery operations: ¥4M
- Marketing/tourism: ¥6M
- Community programs: ¥5M
- **Total: ¥25M/year**

### Financial Viability Analysis

**Break-Even:**
- Studio rents (partial): ¥5M
- Gallery/workshop fees: ¥3M
- Tourism revenue: ¥4M
- Government arts subsidies: ¥8M
- Craft sales tax: ¥3M
- Creative services tax: ¥2M
- **Total: ¥25M** ✓

**Household Economics:**
- Income: ¥500K/month average (high variance)
- Expenses: ¥235K/month
- Savings potential: ¥265K/month (¥3.2M/year)
- **Viable but requires entrepreneurship**

**Risk Level:** Medium-High
- Income highly variable and unpredictable
- Requires entrepreneurial mindset
- Market risk for creative products
- Tourism dependent on marketing

**Mitigation:**
- Select residents with proven income or savings
- Require mix of stable remote workers + craftspeople
- Build strong marketing/tourism strategy early
- Create cooperative sales channels

## Model 5: Agricultural Innovation Town 🌾

### Economic Foundation: Modern Farming Cooperative

**Target Population:** 30 families (60 adults)

### Revenue/Income Sources

#### Individual/Household Income
**Farming Income:**
- New farmers (years 1-3): ¥200-300K/month
- Established farmers (years 4+): ¥350-500K/month
- Specialty crops: ¥400-600K/month
- Agri-tourism: +¥50-150K/month supplement
- Average household: ¥500-700K/month

**Income Sources:**
- 60% crop/livestock sales
- 25% agri-tourism
- 15% value-added products (jams, processing)

### Expenditure/Costs

#### Individual/Household Costs
- Housing: ¥30-50K/month
- Land lease: ¥20-40K/month
- Equipment/supplies: ¥50-100K/month
- Living expenses: ¥100-130K/month
- **Total: ¥200-320K/month**

#### Community Infrastructure Costs
**Initial Setup:**
- Land acquisition/lease: ¥30M
- Equipment pool: ¥25M
- Processing facility: ¥20M
- Farm buildings: ¥15M
- Training center: ¥8M
- **Total: ¥98M**

**Ongoing Annual:**
- Land maintenance: ¥3M
- Equipment maintenance: ¥5M
- Cooperative operations: ¥8M
- Training programs: ¥6M
- Marketing: ¥4M
- **Total: ¥26M/year**

### Financial Viability Analysis

**Break-Even:**
- Cooperative sales: ¥12M margin
- Agri-tourism: ¥6M
- Government agricultural subsidies: ¥8M
- **Total: ¥26M** ✓

**Household Economics:**
- Income: ¥600K/month average (year 4+)
- Expenses: ¥260K/month
- Savings potential: ¥340K/month (¥4M/year)
- **Viable but takes time to ramp up**

**Risk Level:** Medium
- 3-4 year ramp-up to profitability
- Weather/climate risk
- Market price volatility
- Requires farming knowledge/skills

**Mitigation:**
- Government agricultural subsidies (substantial in Japan)
- Crop insurance programs
- Diversified crops reduce single-crop risk
- Agri-tourism provides stable supplementary income
- Cooperative structure shares risk and equipment costs
- Recruit mix of experienced + new farmers

## Funding Strategy

### Government Subsidies Available (Japan, 2025)

**Rural Revitalization Funds:**
- Regional Revitalization Subsidy: Up to ¥10M/project
- Hometown Tax Donation Return Projects: Variable
- MLIT Regional Development: Up to ¥20M

**Sector-Specific:**
- IT Skills Training: ¥2-5M per program
- Eldercare Training: ¥3M per cohort + ongoing support
- Agricultural Start-up: ¥5M per new farmer
- Manufacturing Relocation: ¥10-30M for companies
- Cultural Preservation: ¥5-8M for craft programs

**Housing:**
- Empty House Renovation: ¥1.5M per house
- New Housing Construction (rural): ¥3-5M subsidy per unit

**Total Available per Town:** ¥30-50M over 3 years

### Private Funding

**Company Partnerships:**
- Tech companies: ¥5-10M (talent pipeline investment)
- Manufacturing: ¥50-100M (facility + recruitment)

**Impact Investors:**
- Social impact funds: ¥10-20M
- ESG-focused investors: ¥10-30M

**Crowdfunding:**
- Community crowdfunding: ¥3-5M
- Corporate matching: +¥2M

### Family Contributions

**Refundable Deposits:**
- ¥300-500K per family
- Held in escrow
- Refunded if threshold not met
- Applied to housing/moving if threshold met
- Total from 50 families: ¥15-25M

## Economic Sustainability Long-Term

### Year 5+ Projections

**Tech Training Village:**
- Becomes talent hub, attracts more companies
- Expands to 100+ families
- Possibly spins out startups
- Self-sustaining through co-working fees + company partnerships

**Family Growth Town:**
- Population growth stabilizes town
- Local businesses revive (grocery, services)
- Property values increase
- Tax base supports municipal services
- Becomes model for other aging towns

**Manufacturing Town:**
- If company stable, very sustainable
- Can attract additional manufacturers
- Skilled workforce attracts related industries
- Becomes regional manufacturing hub

**Creative Hub:**
- Builds reputation, attracts more creatives
- Tourism revenue grows
- Gallery sales increase
- Becomes art destination
- May need ongoing modest subsidies

**Agricultural Town:**
- Farms become profitable
- Brand recognition for quality
- Agri-tourism expands
- Younger generation trained
- Fully self-sustaining

## Recommendation: Launch Sequence

**First Town: Family Growth/Eldercare**
- Lowest risk
- Highest government support
- Clearest demand
- Easiest to replicate
- Proves basic model

**Second Town: Tech Training Village**
- Addresses critical shortage
- High income potential
- Different audience
- Tests more complex model
- High visibility

**Third Town: Manufacturing or Agricultural**
- Different economic model
- Geographic diversity
- Demonstrates scalability
- Builds credibility

**Towns 4-5: Creative Hub or other specialized**
- More experimental
- Smaller scale acceptable
- Portfolio of proven models

---

*Each town type is economically viable with appropriate government support and community commitment. The key is matching the right model to the right location and the right group of families.*
